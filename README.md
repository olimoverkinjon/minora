# Minora

A one-tap stacking game inspired by the tiled minarets of Central Asia.

**▶ Play:** https://olimoverkinjon.github.io/minora/

A brick slides back and forth above your tower. Tap (or press Space) to drop it. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced off. Land three perfect drops in a row and the brick grows back. How high can your minaret rise?

- One-tap controls: touch, mouse or keyboard
- Works on phones and desktops, in portrait and landscape
- English and Uzbek (`?lang=en` / `?lang=uz`)
- Built for YouTube Playables: a single `index.html` with no external dependencies besides the Playables SDK
